WELCOME TO FURAMA

THIS WORLD CLASS RESORT, FURAMA DANANG, REPUTABLE FOR BEING A CULINARY RESORT IN VIET NAM

Overlooking the long stretch of wide white sand on Danang Beach, Furama Resort Danang is a gateway to three World Heritage Sites: Hoi An (20 minutes), My Son (90 minutes) and Hue (2 hours). The 198 rooms and suites plus 70 two to four bedroom pool villas feature tasteful décor, designed with traditional Vietnamese style and a touch of French colonial architecture, ensuring the resort’s prestige among royalty, presidents, movie stars, and international business leaders.
